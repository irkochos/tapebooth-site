Nothing leaves your device

Your recordings are saved inside Chrome on your computer, in storage only TapeBooth can reach. TapeBooth has no server, so there is nowhere for them to go.

Read the privacy policy

  • No accountInstall it and record. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No uploads, no analyticsTapeBooth makes no network requests. No ads and no tracking.
  • No permission warningChrome shows no warning when you add it. TapeBooth can't read the sites you visit.
  • You decide what's sharedChrome asks which tab, window or screen to share each time you record, and asks once for your camera and microphone.

From install to a finished video

  1. Allow camera and mic once

    The welcome page asks Chrome for your camera and microphone. Choose “Allow on every visit”, or skip it and record your screen only.

  2. Click the TapeBooth button

    Pick This tab, Window or screen, or Camera only, switch your camera and mic on or off, and press Start recording.

  3. Stop, edit, export

    The editor opens on your recording. Trim, cut and blur, then download it as an MP4 or a GIF.
